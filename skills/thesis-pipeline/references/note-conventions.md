# Writing conventions

## Referencing

The project's style lives in `00-Admin/project-config.md`. Apply it everywhere:
in-text citations, reference list, and any quotation.

Rules that hold in every style:

- A direct quotation always carries a locator: page number, section, paragraph
  marker, or timestamp. No exceptions.
- A work the author has not read directly is cited through the source they did
  read (a secondary citation), and only the consulted source appears in the
  reference list.
- Works never consulted do not appear in the reference list at all.
- Statutes, cases, datasets and web pages follow the same style's rules for
  those source types; check rather than guess.

## Two counts, always distinct

Word limits normally exclude the bibliography, and often exclude footnotes,
appendices and the abstract. Record what the institution excludes in
`project-config.md` and report counts both ways: body only, and total.

## Consistency sweeps

Late in a project, run these checks and fix systematically rather than one by
one:

- One name for each concept (a term of art should not have three synonyms)
- Consistent cross-reference form
- Consistent pronoun convention for the generic person in examples
- No banned constructions (whatever the author has ruled out)
- Both-way citation check: every in-text citation has an entry, every entry is
  cited
