# Session log

Append to `00-Admin/reading-queue.md`. Never rewrite earlier entries: the log is
the audit trail behind the AI declaration and the record of the project's
decisions.

## Entry format

```markdown
## Session log: (date)

**What changed.**
- (Files created or edited, with what was done to them)
- (Notes advanced in status, and on whose confirmation)
- (Decisions the author made, quoted where the wording matters)

**Numbers.** (Word counts against targets, if the draft moved.)

**Flags.** (Raised, cleared, or outstanding. Say which are blocking.)

**Next.** (The immediate next action, specific enough to resume cold.)
```

## Rules

Record decisions in the author's own words where the wording will matter later:
a ruling on referencing, a scope decision, a choice between readings.

Log rejected options too, with the reason. A month later, the author will not
remember why they turned something down.

Where a check was performed, record what was checked, against what, and when.
"Citations verified" is useless; "Kiener pagination confirmed against CrossRef,
17 August" can be relied on.

Write the entry without being asked at the end of a session. If the session was
long, offer to log at natural break points too.
