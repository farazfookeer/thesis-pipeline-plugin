# Contributing

This pipeline came out of one dissertation. It will be wrong in ways that only
show up in other people's fields, so corrections are welcome.

## Useful contributions

- **Field conventions.** The locator systems, edition practices and citation
  habits of your discipline, if they differ from what
  `skills/thesis-pipeline/references/humanities-conventions.md` describes.
- **Tool support.** Conventions for a note-taking or drafting tool not yet
  covered in `references/knowledge-tools.md`.
- **Failure reports.** Cases where a skill let something through that a marker
  or examiner then caught. These are the most valuable reports of all: say what
  the skill did, what it should have done, and what the consequence was.
- **Referencing styles.** The pipeline asks for a style rather than hardcoding
  one, but if a style needs particular handling, say so.

## What this plugin will not do

It will not write anyone's dissertation, generate citations it has not verified,
or advance a source to "read" without the author's own account of it. Proposals
that weaken those gates will be declined - they are the point of the thing.

## How to propose a change

Open an issue describing the problem before writing a pull request, so the
approach can be agreed first. Keep skill bodies short: the working limit is
about 500 words, with detail moved into `references/`.
